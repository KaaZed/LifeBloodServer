Distance tracking technology:
OutDoor activity is recorded by a mini-application 
built into Telegram that sequentially captures the 
user's location from the geo-position broadcast to 
the bot. 
The technology is based on obtaining GPS data, 
recording it and subsequent mathematical 
filtering. LifeBlood takes into account not so much 
steps as the distance traveled on the street.
However, the natively recommended way of 
activity is walking. Therefore, the distance is still 
converted into steps, but the step length is 
averaged for all users at the level of 0.75m \ Step.
So, the main resource spent on the production of 
$LBC is precisely the time of "OutDoor" Activity.
LifeBlood does not control the way you choose to 
travel. The only two conditions fulfilled together 
are a set speed range and a steady GPS signal 
corresponding to an open area.
This technology imposes features due to which 
data accounting anomalies may be observed, 
depending on the quality of coverage, device, 
region and a number of other parameters 
affecting the GPS signal.

The WinToEarn tournament mechanics allows you 
to multiply the amount of $LBC mined
In a model completed for a period, the profit of 
some participants is always achieved at the 
expense of the loss of others. Provided that the 
issue of an asset is limited, within the framework of 
such a model, its value will have an avalanche-like 
character. The main profit is extracted only due to 
the time of entry and exit of capital, and large 
holders become the main beneficiaries.
The Win To Earn mechanics creates transparent 
conditions where it is known that the losers' 
investments will go to the winners, and the result 
depends only on the user's efforts.
The user voluntarily participates in W2E 
tournaments or refuses.W2E


Win2Earn evenly distributes the expense of the 
losing half of the participants as the profit of the 
winning one, according to the conditions:
The prize pot is formed from the amount of tickets 
purchased. Example: a ticket to the tournament 
cost 50 $LBC, and 20 participants of the 
tournament (50*20=1000) the pot of the 
tournament will be 1000 $LBC.
The distribution of awards takes place according 
to the line in the ranking in the tournament, which 
is determined by the achieved result of activity 
per day.
The basic payout coefficient (Kb), a given 
constant equal to 1.95, such a value divides the 
bank in half and leaves 2.5% of the LifeBlood 
commission.
With a value of Kb = 2, the entire collected pot will 
be distributed among the players, and with Kb= 3, 
the project will distribute an additional ~ 30% of 
its funds to those collected from the participants.
The individual payout coefficient (Ki) is calculated 
as: (K-N)/K=Ki Where K is the total number of 
participants and N is the participant's number in 
the rating.
The final award (P) is calculated as: B*Kb*Kv=P The 
price of the entrance ticket (B) sequentially 
multiplied by the basic (Kb) and individual (Ki) 
coefficients.Tournament mechanics:
